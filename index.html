About

hi, i'm emily <3

i've always been a creative person. i love making things and i love experimenting even more, and nails have been the outlet for as long as i can remember. i was doing nail art back when thin brush liners were peak innovation and you genuinely weren't cool unless you had crackle polish.

in 2022 i started my degree, realized i could no longer justify salon prices, and started doing my own gel nails at home. what began as a broke-student workaround turned into kind of an obsession. i've spent the last few years learning how to actually perfect shaping, cuticle care, and most importantly design work. i also found myself wanting to change my nails every couple of days, which is a lot of practice and a lot of wasted potential, so eventually i thought... why not just start offering nails?

so here we are. milk studio is really just a space where i can offer good nails at prices that make sense, in a cozy setup, and get to know some girls on campus while i'm at it. i've kept my pricing as affordable as i possibly can, because being a student shouldn't stop you from being a baddie.

xo, m
