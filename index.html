About

A small studio,
a slower pace.

milk studio is a home-based nail studio in North York, built around one-on-one appointments, clean technique, and designs that feel like you. No rush, no crowded salon floor — just a quiet space to sit down, pick a shape, and leave with hands you actually want to look at.

Every appointment starts with a quick chat about shape, length, and finish, so what you book is exactly what you get.
